🎨 Landing Pages Strategy

High-converting landing pages designed for specific audience segments and campaigns:

Landing Page #1: Main Services

  • Purpose: Showcase core studio services and capabilities
  • Target Audience: Businesses seeking production services
  • Key Elements: Portfolio showcase, service breakdown, testimonials, contact form
  • CTA: "Get a Free Quote" + "View Our Portfolio"
  • Expected CVR: 5-7%

Landing Page #2: Video Production

  • Purpose: Target clients specifically interested in video production
  • Target Audience: Marketing agencies, corporate clients
  • Key Elements: Video samples, process timeline, pricing tiers, ROI calculator
  • CTA: "Schedule a Consultation" + "Download Price Guide"
  • Expected CVR: 6-8%

Landing Page #3: Photography Services

  • Purpose: Capture clients seeking professional photography
  • Target Audience: Real estate, e-commerce, events
  • Key Elements: Before/after gallery, package options, quick turnaround guarantee
  • CTA: "Book a Session" + "See Packages"
  • Expected CVR: 5-6%

Landing Page #4: Post-Production & Editing

  • Purpose: Attract clients needing editing services only
  • Target Audience: Content creators, filmmakers, YouTubers
  • Key Elements: Sample edits, turnaround times, subscription options
  • CTA: "Send Your Footage" + "Start Free Trial"
  • Expected CVR: 7-9%

Landing Page #5: Special Promotions

  • Purpose: Seasonal campaigns and limited-time offers
  • Target Audience: Price-sensitive prospects
  • Key Elements: Countdown timer, discount codes, limited slots, urgency messaging
  • CTA: "Claim Your Discount" + "Limited Spots Available"
  • Expected CVR: 8-12%

Landing Page Best Practices

  • Mobile-first responsive design
  • Load time under 2 seconds
  • Clear value proposition above the fold
  • Trust signals (reviews, certifications, awards)
  • Minimal form fields (3-5 max)
  • Video background or hero video
  • Live chat integration
  • Exit-intent popups
  • Social proof and testimonials
  • Multiple CTA placements

💰 PPC (Pay-Per-Click) Strategy

Data-driven paid advertising campaigns across multiple platforms:

Platform Strategy

Google Ads (60% of budget)

  • Search Campaigns: High-intent keywords ("video production services Dubai", "professional photography studio")
  • Display Network: Retargeting & brand awareness across 2M+ websites
  • YouTube Ads: Video campaigns targeting creative professionals
  • Performance Max: AI-driven campaigns across all Google properties
  • Budget: $6,000/month | Target ROAS: 400%

Meta Ads - Facebook & Instagram (25% of budget)

  • Feed Ads: Showcase portfolio work with engaging captions
  • Story Ads: Behind-the-scenes content and quick testimonials
  • Reels Ads: Short-form video showcasing final products
  • Carousel Ads: Multiple service offerings in single ad
  • Budget: $2,500/month | Target CPA: $30

LinkedIn Ads (10% of budget)

  • Sponsored Content: Thought leadership and case studies
  • InMail Campaigns: Direct outreach to decision-makers
  • Target: Marketing managers, CMOs, agency owners
  • Budget: $1,000/month | Target CPL: $45

TikTok Ads (5% of budget)

  • In-Feed Ads: Creative, engaging content showcasing work
  • Target: Younger demographic, startups, content creators
  • Budget: $500/month | Target CTR: 2.5%

PPC Optimization Tactics

  • Negative Keywords: Continuous refinement to eliminate wasteful spend
  • Ad Schedule Optimization: Bid adjustments based on time-of-day performance
  • Geo-Targeting: Focus on high-value locations with location bid adjustments
  • Device Bidding: Optimize bids for mobile, desktop, tablet separately
  • Audience Layering: Combine intent + demographic targeting
  • Ad Extensions: Sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, call extensions
  • Dynamic Search Ads: Automated ad creation based on website content
  • Smart Bidding: Leverage Google's AI for bid optimization
  • Weekly Performance Reviews: Data-driven adjustments
  • Competitor Conquest: Bid on competitor brand terms strategically
